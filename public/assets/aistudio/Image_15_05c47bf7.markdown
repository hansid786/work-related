## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the understated poise, heritage craftsmanship, and restrained sensuality of high-end atelier jewelry. It addresses an audience of discerning connoisseurs and modern collectors who value timeless design, tactile precious metals, and bespoke curation. 

The aesthetic is grounded in **Minimalist Editorial Luxury**:
- **Generous Canvas & Negative Space:** Content breathes freely through unhurried pacing, avoiding retail clutter or commercial urgency.
- **Editorial Poise:** Visual structures emulate high-fashion monographs and art catalogues, highlighting jewelry silhouettes against warm, tactile backdrops.
- **Materiality & Subtlety:** Restrained metallic warmth, whisper-thin hairline dividing rules, and smooth micro-interactions that feel like sliding a velvet-lined jewelry drawer.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from precious warm metals, raw stones, and fine archival parchment.

- **Primary (`#A17E70` - Rose Taupe):** The signature brand accent derived from the atelier wordmark. Used for primary focus accents, curated story badges, interactive states, and artisanal callouts.
- **Secondary (`#D6BD9A` - Champagne Gold):** A luminous metallic accent used sparingly for gold grade designations, carat highlights, delicate icon highlights, and luxury badges.
- **Tertiary (`#292522` - Deep Charcoal):** The anchor for high-contrast typography, structural keylines, and dominant purchase conversion actions. Replaces harsh true-black with an organic, ink-like depth.
- **Neutral Background (`#FAF7F3` - Warm Ivory):** The primary canvas background, providing warmth and an inviting gallery ambiance.
- **Surface Pure (`#FFFFFF` - Soft White):** Used for elevated product presentation tiles, modal surfaces, and lightbox containers.
- **Neutral Foreground (`#6B6560` - Muted Charcoal):** Dedicated to secondary metadata, specifications, materials details, and breadcrumb navigation.
- **Hairline Border (`#E8E2D9` or `rgba(161, 126, 112, 0.18)`): Used for structural boundaries and delicate dividing lines without creating visual weight.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial tension between the romantic, high-contrast serif displays and the clean, rhythmic geometric sans-serif supporting text.

- **Headlines & Displays (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for collection titles, editorial banners, hero storytelling, and piece nomenclature. Its delicate serifs and dramatic vertical contrast evoke luxury fashion editorial titles.
- **Body & UI Elements (`Montserrat`):** Applied in refined weights (`300` and `400`) to balance airy readability with modern precision.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Handled exclusively with expanded tracking (`0.14em` to `0.18em`) and uppercase treatment for navigation items, collection badges, category tags, and jewelry technical specifications.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is architected around an editorial gallery framework, using disciplined vertical rhythms and generous perimeter margins.

- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Content is framed within wide outer margins (`4rem` up to `6rem` on ultra-wide screens) and `2rem` gutters. Product showcases utilize asymmetric grid offsets (e.g., alternating 7-column editorial imagery with 5-column narrative panels) to mirror boutique window displays.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Gutters condense to `1.5rem`, margins to `2.5rem`. Collections transition cleanly into 2-column or 3-column product cards.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Outer padding scales down to `1.25rem` with `1rem` gutters. Visual cards run full-bleed or use edge-to-edge swipe carousels with subtle visual peeks for next items.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Internal card and container padding prioritizes generous top/bottom balance to keep each jewelry piece isolated from competing visual elements.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy shadows, floating pills, or artificial skeuomorphic depth. Visual hierarchy is achieved through tonal stratification and delicate outlines.

- **Hairline Boundaries:** Separation between headers, collection modules, and gallery drawers relies on subtle `1px solid #E8E2D9` lines or `rgba(161, 126, 112, 0.18)` rules.
- **Tonal Layering:** The base background is Warm Ivory (`#FAF7F3`). Product displays, drawers, and modal overlays step onto Soft White (`#FFFFFF`), naturally distinguishing themselves without drop shadows.
- **Atmospheric Veil Shadow:** For floating cart drawers, modal lightboxes, and quick-view panels, elevation uses an ultra-soft, warm-tinted shadow: `0 20px 48px -12px rgba(41, 37, 34, 0.08)`.
- **Image Frames:** Gemstone and jewelry photography is framed in neutral containers with smooth zoom transitions (`transform: scale(1.03)` with a `600ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` easing curve) upon cursor hover.

## Shapes

The geometric silhouette remains crisp and disciplined. A soft radius of `2px` to `4px` (`roundedness: 1`) is applied across interactive elements to soften hard edges while preserving architectural precision.

- **Buttons & Inputs:** Kept virtually square or softened with a subtle `2px` corner, maintaining tailored poise.
- **Product Tiles & Media Containers:** Strictly sharp (`0px`) or delicately softened (`2px`) to preserve museum-grade presentation frames.
- **Badges & Swatches:** Small luxury tags use `2px` corners; metal and stone sample swatches remain circular for natural authenticity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Dark CTA):** Deep Charcoal background (`#292522`), Warm Ivory text (`#FAF7F3`), `2px` border radius, padding `14px 28px`. Hover shifts background to Rose Taupe (`#A17E70`) with smooth `300ms` transition.
- **Secondary Action (Hairline Outline):** Transparent background, `1px solid #292522` border, Deep Charcoal text. Hover transitions to Deep Charcoal fill and Soft White text.
- **Text Link / Atelier CTA:** Label-caps uppercase typography with an animated hairline underline that expands from center on hover.

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Soft White (`#FFFFFF`) surface set against the Warm Ivory (`#FAF7F3`) canvas, or borderless pure image tile.
- **Imagery:** Aspect ratio 4:5 or 1:1, warm neutral studio lighting, subtle hover zoom (`1.03x`).
- **Product Details:** Centered title in `headline-sm`, subtitle/metal purity in `label-caps` (`#6B6560`), and price in `label-price` (`#292522`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Editorial Badges ("Bespoke", "18k Solid Gold"):** Background `rgba(214, 189, 154, 0.15)`, text Champagne Gold (`#A17E70`), `1px solid rgba(214, 189, 154, 0.3)`, uppercase `label-caps` sizing, padding `4px 10px`.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Framed by a bottom hairline (`1px solid #E8E2D9`) or full minimal border. Focus state shifts the border to Rose Taupe (`#A17E70`) without glow rings. Floating label styling in `body-sm`.
- **Ring Size & Metal Selectors:** Minimalist squared chips with `1px solid #E8E2D9` border. Selected state receives a Deep Charcoal border and fill with white text, or an active Rose Taupe highlight ring.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Selection Controls:** `16px` diameter circles or squares with a `1px` Deep Charcoal border. Active state features a refined inner dot in Rose Taupe or Deep Charcoal.

### Modals & Drawers (Bespoke Additions)
- **Slide-out Cart & Atelier Consultation Drawer:** Slides in smoothly from the right with a Warm Ivory background, framed with hairline horizontal rules separating line items and estimated shipping details.
